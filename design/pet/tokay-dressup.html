Same pet, different nights out.

How this becomes the app's pet. Each part is its own layer: tail, legs, body, arms, head, eyelids, mouth, then item slots for hand, head, face, neck and bag. In the app:
  1. Items come only from real check-ins, using what Paisaidee already records: the type of place, the time, riverside, province.
  2. Some items level up: the coffee goes paper cup → ceramic mug → latte-art cup, so going back to cafés keeps paying off.
  3. The gecko grows at levels 5, 10 and 20. Level comes from embers, the same as the level bar on the You page.
  4. You choose what to wear from what you've earned. Nothing can be bought.
The buttons above are a simulator: each one counts as a check-in at a new place (+25 embers).
