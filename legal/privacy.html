The short version

  • You can browse Paisaidee without an account. Nothing about you is stored on our side when you do.
  • Your location is used on your phone to sort places by distance. It only reaches us when you check in or ask for directions.
  • If you sign in with Google, we keep your Google account ID and a username. We don't keep your email address.
  • Your check-ins, ratings and rankings are visible only to you. Public numbers like "8 people this month" are counts, never names.
  • No ads, no selling data, no tracking cookies.

Who we are

Paisaidee (ໄປໃສດີ) is a guide to cafés, bars and events in Vientiane, Lao PDR, run by [operator]. Questions about this policy go to [contact email].

What we collect

When you browse without an account. We don't create any record of you. Your saved places, theme and whether you've seen the intro are kept in your browser or the app on your phone, not on our servers. When you pick a mood in the intro, we count which mood was picked, whether it was day or night, and whether the person was signed in. That count has no name or ID attached.

When you sign in with Google. Google confirms who you are and gives us an ID number for your Google account. We make a username from your Google name (for example @kar). We don't store your email address, contacts or Google password. A session token keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, or until you sign out.

When you check in. We store which place, when, the embers it earned, and the position your phone reported at that moment, which is how we confirm you're really there. We clear that position after 90 days. The check-in itself stays as part of your history.

Your progress. Embers, level, badges, monthly challenges and your "How was it?" ratings and rankings.

A profile photo, if you add one. It's stored with Cloudinary, our photo service.

If you list a venue. The details and photos you submit. Once we approve them they're public, because that's the point of a listing.

Location

Paisaidee asks for your location to sort places by distance and to show directions. You can say no and still use everything except "near me", directions and check-ins. Your position is used on your device. It's sent to us only when you check in, or when you ask for directions. For directions, we pass the start and end points (rounded to about 10 metres) to OpenRouteService to work out the route.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your check-ins, embers, level, badges, challenges and rankings.
  • To stop cheating, such as check-ins from somewhere you're not.
  • To show counts on a place, like "8 people this month" or the busy-times chart. The busy-times chart only appears once a place has at least 10 check-ins from at least 5 different people, so no one person can be picked out.

Who can see what

Your check-ins, ratings, rankings and badges are visible only to you. There's no public leaderboard. Your username and photo aren't shown to other people today. If we add friends later, you'll choose what friends can see before anything is shared.

How long we keep it

  • Check-in positions: 90 days.
  • Your account, check-ins and progress: until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: 30 days at most.

Your choices

  • Turn location off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Sign out at any time from the You tab.
  • To delete your account and everything linked to it, email [contact email] from the Google account you signed in with. We'll confirm when it's done.
